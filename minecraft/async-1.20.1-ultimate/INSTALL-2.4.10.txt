Installation
Put one copy of the HariMultiThread-Ultimate-1.20.1-2.4.10-vulkan-hybrid.jar in your Forge profile's mods directory. Replace the previous HariMultiThread/Async JAR. The Forge Vulkan renderer is included; remove a separate VulkanMod JAR. Keep the normal dependencies of your additional mods. Back up your world before changing any modpack.

C2ME Forge is optional. Original unmodified 0.2.0-forge.9.6 (CurseForge file 8929972) and 0.2.0-forge.9.8 (file 9040801) passed the recorded profiles. The broad 13-profile matrix uses 9.6; 9.8 has focused Aether+Midnight, OptiFine and Distant Horizons checks. All third-party JARs retain their original bytes.

FPS measurement
Press Ctrl+F9 while playing in a loaded world. The capture warms up for five seconds, then records thirty seconds of all frame intervals. The saved report is harimt-fps-last.json in the Minecraft instance folder. Save a copy before starting another capture because the next report replaces it.

Compare average FPS, 1% lows and p95/p99 frame times together. Use the same world, camera, mods, window size, resource/shader packs and render/simulation distances. Let chunks and shaders settle first. Alternate at least three runs per build. For maximum-throughput comparisons use unlimited FPS and VSync off on both builds; for everyday smoothness use your ordinary identical pacing settings. Menus, reloads, dimension changes and graphics/pacing changes invalidate a capture rather than silently dropping slow frames.

The 1% low is 1000 divided by the mean of the slowest ceil(1% of samples) frame times. Average FPS is 1000 divided by the mean of all frame times. This measures actual rendered-frame intervals including pacing and driver waits, rather than GPU execution time. Capture is idle during normal gameplay and does not sample clocks or allocate frame arrays.

Rendering and quality
The new texture staging copies precisely the byte span consumed by each Vulkan image upload. Every requested pixel, animation update, mip level, transition and transfer barrier remains. Bounds and overflow checks prevent reads beyond the source buffer, and its position is preserved. There are no animation skips or changes to resolution, graphics settings, view distance or simulation.

Compatible profiles use Vulkan rendering. External renderer/shader integrations and Fabulous graphics retain the established complete OpenGL compatibility path; async processing and the separate Vulkan collision backend remain available. The GL contract recognizes 111 exact overloads and selects compatibility rendering for unsupported external shader/compute operations and 2,248 unsupported overloads.
